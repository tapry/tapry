# Code of conduct

OpenTake follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the
person, and make room for people who are new. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted anywhere the project lives (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat).

To report something, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's security
advisory form ("Report a vulnerability" under the Security tab works for conduct reports too),
or by direct message to a maintainer. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may warn,
remove content, or ban people who don't follow this code.
