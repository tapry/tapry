Participant names in the demo are fictional, and do not identify the performers.

The stock media is licensed separately from the application source code.
Excerpts are cropped to 4:3, resized to 480 × 360, stripped of audio and metadata,
and encoded as H.264 MP4 with fast-start playback. WebP posters are extracted from
the same excerpts for the initial frame, reduced motion and playback failures.
