# 07 — Go-to-Market

## Positioning lines
- "The remote recording studio you own."
- "Every guest recorded locally. Never lost. Stored where you choose."
- For organisations: "The interview recording studio your ethics board will approve." / "Recordings and AI that never leave your servers."
- For switchers: "Switching from Riverside or SquadCast? Keep separate tracks. Use any editor."
- **Never:** "cheaper Riverside."

## Before launch (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)
- Landing page (Cap structure, Dub polish — see `10-design-inspiration.md`) + cloud waitlist + GitHub CTA.
- **Build in public on LinkedIn and X**, 2–3 posts/week: recording tech clips, reliability tests, "we killed a guest's tab mid-recording — here's what happened".
- Recruit **20 alpha users**: 10 podcasters/interviewers (r/podcasting, podcast Discords, small YouTube interview channels) + **10 researchers/journalists** (research methods groups, university media labs, newsroom tech people).
- **10 validation interviews with research teams/university IT** (see `01` "Still to validate").
- Before/after demo: Zoom recording vs Tapry.

## Organisation motion (from month 3–4, i.e., Apr 2027)
- **Targets:** research groups (qualitative research, UX research), university IT/ethics offices, newsrooms, EU companies with data-residency needs, health/legal/HR training teams.
- **Offer:** free pilot (30 days) → Research team plan or organisation licence; security pack (data-flow diagram, DPA, subprocessors list, retention controls, self-host option).
- **Outreach:** LinkedIn (you're comfortable with calls/outreach for this), ethics office contacts, research methods mailing lists, conference talks/posters on secure remote interviewing.
- Goal: **3 pilots by end of April 2027**, **3 paying organisations by Sep 2027**.

## Metrics
- GitHub stars, Docker pulls, active self-hosted installs (opt-in ping).
- Cloud: sign-up → first recording → second recording (activation) → paid.
- **Recording success rate (must be ~100%)**, time to 100% uploaded after Stop, alerts fired per session.
- Organisation pipeline: interviews → pilots → paid.
- MRR, churn, lifetime deals sold.
