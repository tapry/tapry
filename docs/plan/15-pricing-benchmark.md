**Price per included hour**, yearly billing (monthly in brackets): Descript Creator 0.80–1.60 (1.17–2.33) · Boomcaster Creator 1.53 (1.93) · Riverside Pro 1.60 (1.93) at 15 h, 4.80 (5.80) at 5 h · Riverside Grow 1.70 (1.95) · Boomcaster Pro 2.10 (2.63) · Iris Studio 2.61 (2.90) · Iris Starter 4.05 (4.50).

**What this says**
- The entry band for a metered plan is $15–24 a month billed yearly, for 5–15 hours and one seat. $9 buys 2 hours (Iris).
- The upgrade from the first paid plan is driven by hours, then seats or studios. Quality is rarely the gate.
- Yearly discounts are about 20% (range 10–40%).
- "Free" is taken: Zencastr, Cleanfeed Lite, StreamYard's and Zoom's unmetered plans.
- No vendor page offers self-hosting, customer-owned storage or data residency. SSO exists only on quote-only tiers and Zoom Business.

**What this says**
- The free edition keeps the product; payment buys SSO, audit log, permissions, branding removal and a support promise. Plausible, which sells no support, gets donations only.
- The smallest paid self-hosting contract is about $500–750 a year (10 seats). Larger ones are per seat or by quote.
- Education and non-profit discounts: 30% is the common level among small vendors (Outline, Cap); large vendors give 50–85%.
- Fixed-price installation packages: none published.

## Launch offers

- **Plane One** ($799 lifetime licence): several hundred thousand dollars, then withdrawn. Support was uneven (one customer needing as much as 25) and under 5% of buyers moved to a subscription.
- **Documenso early adopter**: a capped subscription, $30 a month, 100 seats, filled in about a year (*secondary*).
- Lifetime-deal founders report that buyers almost never upgrade (*secondary*, unaudited).

So: a one-time offer only for something that costs nothing to serve (own storage, community support), and a capped price lock for the hosted plans.

## Gaps in this benchmark
- Riverside's hours contradict each other on its own page; its page carried a staging notice.
- Async, Restream and Cleanfeed figures are partly unresolved (pages render with scripts).
- No count exists of newsrooms, research groups or public bodies that would buy a recorder: the size of that segment is unknown.
